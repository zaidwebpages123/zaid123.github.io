LYALLPUR SCHOOL OF THOUGHTS — GOOGLE-READY WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: website with SEO metadata and School structured data
- sitemap.xml: sitemap for Google
- robots.txt: tells search engines they may crawl the site

IMPORTANT:
1. Replace every https://example.com/ with your real website URL in:
   - index.html (canonical + Open Graph URL)
   - robots.txt
   - sitemap.xml
2. Upload all three files to the ROOT of your hosting account.
3. After the site is live, add the site in Google Search Console and submit:
   https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/sitemap.xml
4. Google indexing can take time and search appearance/ranking is not guaranteed.
